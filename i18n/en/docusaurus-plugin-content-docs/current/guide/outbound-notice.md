# Creating Outbound Notices

An Outbound Notice (RW order) is used for manual outbound operations outside of platform orders — such as B2B shipments, sample dispatches, etc.

## Process

**Path:** SCM → Inventory Documents → Outbound Notices → Click **[New]**

1. Select the outbound type and fill in consignee information
2. Select the products to ship and enter quantities
3. Add any special instructions in the remarks field
4. Click **[Save]** → Click **[Approve]**

After approval, copy the **RW order number** and send it to the relevant group to notify the warehouse.

:::caution
Once approved, the outbound notice is sent to the warehouse. To make changes, cancel the approval first or contact warehouse staff.
:::
