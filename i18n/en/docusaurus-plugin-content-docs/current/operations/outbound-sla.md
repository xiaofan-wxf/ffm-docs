## Loss & Damage Compensation

**Warehouse loss:** Warehouse is liable after the contractual deductible threshold is met.

**Courier loss:**
- Flash Express: Warehouse assists with claim coordination
- Other couriers: Customer handles claim directly with courier

**Damage:**
- Items ≤ 2,000 THB: Monthly deductible of 0.05% of inventory value; warehouse covers the rest
- Items > 2,000 THB: Optional premium insurance available (0.6% of declared value), full coverage by warehouse
