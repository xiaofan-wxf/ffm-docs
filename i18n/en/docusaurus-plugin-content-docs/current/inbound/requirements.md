# Packing Standards & Rejection Rules

## Packing List Requirements

- Each box must have a packing list label attached per standard
- Mixed-SKU boxes are not accepted
- If multiple SKUs are in one box, clear physical separation is required

## Rejection Criteria

The warehouse will reject shipments that meet any of the following conditions:

1. No inbound order created, or warehouse not notified in advance
2. Delivery outside receiving hours (1 day before / 2 days after promotions, Sundays, public holidays)
3. Quantity on delivery note does not match actual goods (assessed by warehouse)
4. Visible damage to outer packaging or inner contents
5. No packing list on outer box showing inbound order number and SKU details

## Packaging Services

For value-added services such as labeling or repackaging, please contact your account manager for a quote. Fees are charged per contract terms.
