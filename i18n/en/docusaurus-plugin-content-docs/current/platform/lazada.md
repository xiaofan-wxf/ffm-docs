# Lazada Store Authorization

## Authorization Steps

1. Log in to the Lazada Seller Center → Click **Service Market**

![Service Market Entry](/img/img_006.png)

2. Search for `Flash` → Find the **FLASH WMS** application

![Search Flash WMS](/img/img_007.png)
![Flash WMS Application](/img/img_008.png)

3. Select [Authorize Only] → Set period to **6 months** → Click [Authorize]

![Select Authorize Only](/img/img_009.png)

4. Check "I agree and have signed" → Click [Confirm] → "Subscription successful" message appears

![Agree and Confirm](/img/img_011.png)

5. Go back to SCM → Settings → Store Profile → Select the store → Click [Authorize]

![SCM Store Profile Authorization](/img/img_012.png)

6. Select country → Enter store login email and password → Click "Submit"

![Authorization Success](/img/img_013.png)

## Post-Authorization Setup

After successful authorization, update the **shipping address** in the Lazada Seller Center to the Flash warehouse address.

![Warehouse Address Setup Example](/img/img_014.png)

SCM Store Profile → Click [Edit] → Platform Warehouse Settings:
- Click [Get Platform Warehouses]
- Click [Edit] on the right → Select Flash warehouse
- Fetch orders from this warehouse: **Yes**
- Check the corresponding FFM warehouse → Save

## FAQ

### DOF Parcel Cannot Be Collected

**Reason:** When a new Lazada store has fewer than 10 daily orders on average, the platform requires the customer to self-deliver parcels to the courier drop-off point.

**To apply for Pickup collection:** Please contact your account manager to submit a request.

**Temporary workaround before approval:** Deliver parcels to the courier drop-off point. Staff incentives can be paid through the petty cash account and will be included in the monthly billing.

![Apply for Pickup](/img/img_063.png)

### Lazada Quality Inspection Warehouse Integration

If you need to integrate with the Lazada quality inspection warehouse, please contact your project manager for the dedicated process documentation.
